09 / QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Good to know.

Clear answers, without the fine-print fog.

01 What is ARTHNIX? +
ARTHNIX is an AI-powered business management platform designed to bring billing, inventory, sales, accounting, payroll, and everyday operations into one simple experience.
02 Can I use the free business tools without signing up? +
Yes. The public calculators and generators are designed to be useful without creating an account. Generated documents should be reviewed before downloading.
03 Does ARTHNIX support GST invoices? +
GST billing and invoice workflows are part of the ARTHNIX product direction. Specific tax assumptions, fields, and supported formats will be clearly documented as each feature is released.
04 Can I manage inventory and billing together? +
The platform is designed around connected billing and inventory workflows. Capabilities are released only after the full flow has been implemented and tested.
05 Does ARTHNIX work on mobile? +
The experience is designed to work in a browser on desktop and mobile. No installation is required to explore the current site or free tools.
06 How does the AI assistant work? +
The planned assistant will help organize supported business tasks from plain-language requests. Important details remain reviewable, and the assistant must not invent GSTINs, tax rates, invoice numbers, or other critical information.
07 Is my business data secure? +
Security and privacy practices will be documented alongside the relevant product capabilities. The product direction emphasizes permission-aware access and user review of important actions.
08 Is ARTHNIX suitable for small businesses? +
Yes. ARTHNIX is designed for Indian MSMEs, retailers, service providers, restaurants, and growing businesses that want a simpler starting point.
09 What happens when I need more features? +
Start with free tools and essential workflows, then move to a plan that fits your business as more connected capabilities become available.
