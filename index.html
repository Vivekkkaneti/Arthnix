Good to know

A few things you might want to know.

A little clarity before you get started.

ARTHNIX is being developed as an AI-powered business management platform designed to simplify invoicing, inventory, sales, and other everyday business operations.
The platform is intended for small and medium-sized businesses, retailers, service providers, restaurants, and growing businesses.
The goal is to make suitable public calculators and document generators available without requiring an account wherever practical. Individual tools will explain any requirements that apply.
Connected billing and inventory are part of the product vision. The available capabilities will be identified clearly as the platform is implemented.
The planned experience uses natural language to help organize supported business tasks. Important information must remain reviewable, and users should confirm critical details before finalizing documents or transactions.
This website is a starting point for the ARTHNIX product vision. Individual tools and business features must be implemented and tested before they are presented as available for use.
