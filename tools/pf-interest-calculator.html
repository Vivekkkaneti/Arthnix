How It Works

Understand your PF interest calculation

Your provident fund balance can grow through regular contributions and interest. The calculator estimates both using the values you enter.

01

Enter your current balance

Start with your current EPF balance. Your EPFO passbook is the best place to check the employee and employer EPF amounts.

02

Add monthly contributions

Enter the employee contribution and the employer contribution credited to EPF. The employer's EPS allocation is generally separate.

03

Choose your projection

Set the annual interest assumption and number of years. You can also model contribution increases and annual withdrawals.

How this estimate is calculated

This calculator uses a simplified annual projection. It estimates the opening balance's interest and the interest earned on contributions added throughout each year.

Estimated interest = Opening balance × annual rate + contribution interest estimate

Monthly contributions are approximated as being spread across the year. The calculator is designed for planning and comparison; it does not replicate EPFO's official monthly interest-crediting method.

Frequently Asked Questions

PF interest calculator FAQs

Learn how EPF interest works and what to keep in mind when estimating your provident fund balance.

What is EPF interest?

EPF interest is the interest credited to eligible balances in an employee's provident fund account at the rate declared for the relevant financial year. The rate may change from year to year.

How is PF interest calculated in India?

EPFO applies its prescribed method using eligible monthly balances and the applicable annual interest rate. This tool uses a simplified projection to help with planning and does not reproduce the official monthly interest calculation.

Is the EPF interest rate fixed?

No. The rate is declared for the relevant financial year and can change. Verify the applicable rate using official EPFO announcements before relying on a calculation.

Does the employer's full PF contribution earn EPF interest?

The employer's contribution may be divided between EPF and the Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS), subject to applicable rules. For a more accurate estimate, use the actual EPF portion shown in your salary slip or EPFO passbook rather than the full employer contribution if part is allocated to EPS.

Can I calculate my PF balance after 20 or 30 years?

Yes. You can select a projection period of up to 50 years. Longer projections are hypothetical because future interest rates, contributions, employment, and withdrawals may change.

Will the result match my EPFO passbook exactly?

No. The calculator provides an estimate. Actual balances may differ due to contribution dates, monthly interest calculations, rate changes, withdrawals, transfers, rounding, and account adjustments.

Is this PF Interest Calculator free?

Yes. You can use this calculator in your browser without creating an account. Your calculations are performed locally in the page.
