Time card calculation formula

The calculator determines the elapsed time between clock-in and clock-out, subtracts unpaid breaks, and separates the remaining hours into regular and overtime categories.

Shift duration = Clock-out − Clock-in
Net worked time = Shift duration − Unpaid break
Regular hours = The lower of net worked hours or your regular-hours threshold
Overtime hours = Net worked hours − Regular hours, when positive
Regular pay = Regular hours × Hourly rate
Overtime pay = Overtime hours × Hourly rate

If the shift crosses midnight, the calculator treats the clock-out time as occurring on the following day. The selected rounding option is applied to net worked time before regular and overtime hours are separated.

Frequently Asked Questions

Time card calculator FAQs.

What is a time card calculator?

A time card calculator helps estimate how long you worked during a shift. It can subtract unpaid breaks, identify hours above a chosen regular-hours threshold, and estimate pay when you provide an hourly rate.

Can I calculate an overnight shift?

Yes. Select “Yes — ends the next day” when your shift crosses midnight. You can also use automatic detection, which treats an end time earlier than the start time as an overnight shift. For a shift starting and ending at the same clock time, choose the appropriate option carefully.

Are breaks automatically deducted?

No. Enter your unpaid break duration in minutes. The calculator subtracts that amount from the total shift duration. If your workplace treats a break as paid time, do not include it in the unpaid-break field.

How does this calculator determine overtime?

It compares net worked hours with the regular-hours threshold you enter. Any net hours above that threshold are displayed as overtime. Actual overtime eligibility and overtime pay rates depend on applicable law, employment terms, and workplace policy.

Does the pay estimate include overtime premiums or deductions?

No. The estimate uses the hourly rate you enter for both regular and overtime hours. It does not add an overtime premium or subtract tax, insurance, or other payroll deductions. Actual payroll may therefore differ.

Can I round my work hours?

Yes. Choose exact minutes or rounding to the nearest 5, 10, or 15 minutes. Employer timekeeping rules may require a particular method, so check your policy before using rounded results for official timesheets.

Are my time card entries uploaded?

This calculator performs its calculations in your browser. It does not send the entered times or hourly rate to a calculation server.

Can I save or print my time card summary?

Yes. Select “Print / PDF” to open your browser's print dialog. From there, you can print the result or select a PDF destination if your browser and device support it. The printed view focuses on the calculated results.
