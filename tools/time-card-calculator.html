How it works

Understand your time card.

A time card records when you start and finish work, along with unpaid breaks. The calculator totals your net working hours and provides an estimated pay breakdown based on your settings.

01

Enter clock-in and clock-out

Add the start and finish time for each day you worked. Overnight shifts are supported when the end time falls after midnight.

02

Subtract unpaid breaks

03

Review hours and pay

View total hours, regular hours, overtime hours, and estimated gross earnings. Copy or print your summary for your records.

Calculation method.

The calculator uses the following general approach. Your workplace may use different rules for overtime, shift premiums, or rounding.

How hours and pay are estimated

Elapsed shift time = clock-out time − clock-in time (with overnight shifts continuing into the next day)
Net daily hours = elapsed shift time − unpaid break time
Regular hours = the lesser of total weekly hours and the configured regular-hours limit
Overtime hours = total weekly hours − regular hours
Estimated gross pay = regular hours × hourly rate + overtime hours × hourly rate × overtime multiplier

This tool uses a weekly overtime threshold entered by you. Some jurisdictions calculate overtime daily, by pay period, or according to other rules. The estimate does not determine legal entitlement to overtime or replace an employer's official payroll calculation.

Frequently asked questions.

What is a time card calculator?

A time card calculator totals the hours recorded for work shifts. It can account for clock-in and clock-out times, unpaid breaks, regular hours, overtime, and estimated earnings.

Can I calculate a shift that ends after midnight?

Yes. Enter the clock-in time and an earlier clock-out time. The calculator treats the clock-out as occurring on the following day. For example, 22:00 to 06:00 represents an eight-hour shift before break deductions.

Are unpaid breaks included in total work hours?

No. The break minutes you enter are subtracted from each shift's elapsed duration. Enter zero if you did not take an unpaid break. Paid-break treatment can vary by employer and local rules.

How is overtime calculated?

This calculator compares your total weekly net hours against the regular-hours threshold you enter. Hours above that threshold are shown as overtime and multiplied by your selected overtime rate. Your applicable legal or employer overtime rules may differ.

Does estimated gross pay equal my take-home salary?

No. Gross pay is an estimate before taxes, deductions, benefits, allowances, and other payroll adjustments. The actual amount paid may differ.

Can I save or share the time card?

Use Copy Summary to copy the calculated details, or choose Print / Save PDF to print the results or save them as a PDF using your browser's print dialog.
