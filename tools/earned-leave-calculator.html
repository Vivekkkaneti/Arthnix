How it works

Estimate encashment

Enter your monthly salary and the applicable salary divisor to estimate the gross value of eligible leave days.

Earned leave formula

Accrued leave = Eligible days worked ÷ Days worked per leave day
Closing balance = Opening balance + Accrued leave − Leave taken + Adjustments
Daily salary estimate = Monthly salary ÷ Selected salary divisor
Gross encashment estimate = Eligible encashment days × Daily salary estimate

The calculator applies your selected rounding method to the accrual result. If you enable a maximum accrual cap and enter a cap value, the accrued leave will not exceed that cap.

Important: Leave rules vary by employer and location.

Earned leave, also called privilege leave in some workplaces, may be governed by applicable legislation, establishment type, employment contract, and company policy. Accrual rules, eligible days, carry-forward limits, encashment eligibility, salary components, and calculation methods can differ. Confirm the applicable rules with your HR team before relying on a final entitlement or payment figure.

Frequently asked questions

Earned leave calculator FAQs.

Answers to common questions about leave accrual and balance estimates.

What is earned leave?
Earned leave is paid time off accumulated under an applicable employment policy or legal framework. It may also be called privilege leave in some workplaces. The exact entitlement and accrual method depend on the rules that apply to your employment.
How do I calculate earned leave?
A simple estimate divides eligible days worked by the number of eligible days required to earn one leave day. For example, under a hypothetical rule of one leave day per 20 eligible days, 240 eligible days would generate 12 leave days before any applicable cap or rounding adjustment.
Are weekends and holidays included in eligible days worked?
That depends on the applicable policy or law. Some rules use days worked, some consider days of service or attendance, and some define specific exclusions. Enter the eligible day count according to your organization's actual rule.
Can I calculate leave encashment with this tool?
Yes. Enter your monthly salary, select a salary divisor, and optionally specify the number of days eligible for encashment. The tool calculates an indicative gross amount. Your actual payment may depend on the applicable salary components, eligibility rules, limits, deductions, and tax treatment.
Why is my company's leave balance different?
Your employer may use a different accrual rule, rounding method, eligibility period, carry-forward limit, adjustment process, or salary calculation. This calculator is a general estimator and does not access your HR or payroll records.
Does this calculator store my salary information?
The calculations on this page run in your browser. The calculator does not intentionally send your entered salary or leave details to a calculation server. Avoid entering confidential information on devices or browsers you do not trust.
