Understand the Calculation

GST made simple.

Goods and Services Tax is an indirect tax applied to many goods and services in India. The calculation depends on the applicable rate and whether the entered amount already includes GST.

01

GST Exclusive

Use this when the amount is before GST. The calculator calculates tax on the entered value and adds it to determine the final amount.

02

GST Inclusive

Use this when the entered price already includes GST. The calculator separates the taxable value and tax component from the total.

03

CGST, SGST & IGST

For an eligible intra-state supply, GST is generally split into CGST and SGST. For an eligible inter-state supply, IGST generally applies.

GST calculation formulas

GST Exclusive: GST = Taxable Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100
Final Amount (Exclusive): Taxable Amount + GST
GST Inclusive: Taxable Amount = Inclusive Amount ÷ (1 + GST Rate ÷ 100)
Intra-state: CGST = GST ÷ 2   SGST = GST ÷ 2
Inter-state: IGST = Total GST
Important: This calculator provides an estimate for general informational purposes. GST rates, exemptions, place-of-supply rules and tax treatment depend on the specific goods or services and applicable law. Confirm the appropriate rate and treatment before issuing an invoice or filing a tax return.
