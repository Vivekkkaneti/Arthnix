  • Choose a sequence length that allows your business to grow.
  • Keep an appropriate record of issued and cancelled invoices.

Understand the Basics

Simple numbering.
Clearer records.

An invoice number helps identify an invoice and distinguish it from other business documents. A consistent format can make invoices easier to organize, retrieve, and reconcile.

What is an invoice number?

An invoice number is a unique identifier assigned to an invoice. Businesses use it to reference transactions, track payments, and maintain organized records.

Why use a prefix?

A prefix such as INV can help distinguish invoices from other documents. Businesses may choose prefixes based on their own internal numbering practices.

Why include a date?

A year or month component can make invoice numbers easier to group by period. Choose a format that remains clear and consistent across your records.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about

Learn how invoice numbering works and how to use this free generator.

Is this invoice number generator free?
Yes. You can generate invoice numbers, copy them, and export generated batches as CSV without paying to use this tool.
Can I customize the invoice number format?
Yes. You can customize the prefix, separator, date component, starting sequence, sequence digit length, and number of invoice numbers to generate.
Can I generate multiple invoice numbers at once?
Yes. Set the number of entries you need and generate a batch. The tool supports batches of up to 500 numbers at a time.
Does the tool remember my sequence?
When the remember-sequence option is enabled, the tool stores sequence information in your browser's local storage. This is browser-specific and is not a shared or centrally managed invoice register. Clearing browser data may remove it.
Can I export the invoice numbers?
Yes. Use the Download CSV button to export the generated batch. You can open the resulting file in spreadsheet applications that support CSV files.
Does generating a number create an invoice?
No. This tool creates invoice-number suggestions only. It does not create an invoice document, record a sale, collect payment, or submit tax information.
Is a generated format automatically compliant with Indian requirements?
No. The generator does not verify compliance with applicable invoicing requirements. Check the rules relevant to your business and transaction, and consult a qualified professional when necessary.

You can explore the ARTHNIX Invoice Generator to prepare an invoice with business details, customer details, items, tax information, and other invoice fields.
