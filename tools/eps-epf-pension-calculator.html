Know Your Benefits

Understanding EPF and EPS

EPF and EPS are related employee social-security schemes, but they serve different purposes.

₹

Employee Provident Fund (EPF)

EPF is a retirement savings arrangement. Contributions and applicable interest accumulate in the provident fund account. The actual balance depends on contributions, interest credits, transfers and withdrawals.

↗

Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS)

EPS provides pension benefits to eligible members. The standard pension formula commonly uses pensionable salary multiplied by eligible pensionable service and divided by 70, subject to the applicable scheme provisions and adjustments.

✓

Employer contributions

The employer's PF contribution is allocated between EPF and EPS where applicable. The allocation is governed by scheme rules, wage limits, membership conditions and any applicable higher-wage arrangements.

Frequently Asked Questions

EPF and EPS pension questions

General information to help you interpret your estimates.

What is the difference between EPF and EPS?

EPF is a provident fund savings scheme in which contributions accumulate with applicable interest. EPS is a pension scheme that can provide a monthly pension to eligible members under its rules. The two benefits are calculated differently.

How is the EPS monthly pension commonly estimated?

A commonly used formula is pensionable salary multiplied by pensionable service, divided by 70. The result depends on the applicable scheme provisions, pensionable salary rules, service records and eligibility. This calculator is only an estimate.

Is EPS pension available after 10 years of service?

Ten years of eligible pensionable service is generally an important threshold for eligibility for a regular EPS pension. Special circumstances, age, service records and applicable rules can affect the benefit. Confirm your position with EPFO before relying on an estimate.

At what age can I generally receive EPS pension?

The standard age for a monthly EPS pension is generally 58. Reduced pension may be available in qualifying circumstances from an earlier age, subject to applicable scheme provisions. The calculator does not model every early-pension adjustment.

Does this calculator use the official EPFO balance?

No. It uses the values you enter to produce a simplified estimate. It does not access your EPFO account, passbook, employer payroll records or UAN information.

Why might my actual EPF balance be different?

Your official balance can differ because of actual monthly contributions, contribution wage limits, interest-credit timing, salary changes, transfers, withdrawals, missing deposits and other account-specific adjustments. The calculator assumes a simplified contribution and compounding schedule.

Does this calculator store my salary or personal details?

The calculator runs in your browser and does not send your entries to ARTHNIX through this page. Avoid sharing or exporting your report on a device or account that other people can access.
