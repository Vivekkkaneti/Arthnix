Understand Your Benefits

EPF and EPS are related,
but they work differently.

EPF is a retirement savings account funded by employee and employer contributions and credited interest. EPS is a pension scheme funded through the employer's contribution allocation under applicable rules. Your EPF balance and EPS pension therefore need separate estimates.

01 / EPF

Employees' Provident Fund

EPF accumulates eligible contributions and interest in your provident fund account. Your actual balance depends on contributions, withdrawals, transfers, interest credits, and account history.

02 / EPS

Employees' Pension Scheme

EPS provides pension benefits for eligible members. A commonly used simplified formula uses pensionable salary multiplied by eligible pensionable service and divided by 70. Official calculations can involve additional rules and conditions.

03 / RETIREMENT

Plan with realistic assumptions

Your final EPF amount and EPS pension may depend on historical wages, membership dates, eligible service, withdrawals, contribution records, and the rules applicable to your membership.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about
EPF and EPS.

Learn what the calculator estimates and what you should verify before making retirement or pension decisions.

What is the difference between EPF and EPS?

EPF is a provident fund that builds a balance through eligible contributions and interest. EPS is a pension scheme that may provide a recurring pension to eligible members under its rules. The EPF balance is not the same as the EPS pension entitlement.

How is the monthly EPS pension estimated?

A commonly referenced simplified formula is pensionable salary multiplied by pensionable service and divided by 70. This page uses that formula for an illustrative estimate. Your official pension can differ due to the applicable salary calculation, eligible service, membership history, and current EPFO provisions.

Does the calculator guarantee my retirement EPF balance?

No. It projects a possible balance based on the current balance, assumed interest rate, monthly salary, contribution rates, and remaining service. Actual contributions, interest credits, withdrawals, transfers, salary changes, and account-specific details may change the result.

Why does pensionable salary matter?

EPS pension calculations use pensionable salary under the applicable scheme rules, which may not be identical to your full monthly salary. Historical membership, applicable wage ceilings, and higher-pension arrangements can affect the salary figure used.

Can I use this calculator if I have less than 10 years of service?

You can use the calculator to explore an illustrative formula result, but a numerical estimate does not establish eligibility for a monthly pension. Pension eligibility and any other benefit depend on applicable EPS rules and your service record. Confirm your position with EPFO.

Does this calculator connect to my UAN or EPF account?

No. Do not enter your UAN, password, OTP, or other account credentials here. You must enter the figures yourself. The calculation is performed in your browser and is not submitted to ARTHNIX.

Where can I verify my official EPF and pension details?

Check your official EPF passbook, member portal, employer records, and EPFO guidance. Use official sources for eligibility, pension claims, withdrawals, transfers, and any higher-pension application or calculation.
