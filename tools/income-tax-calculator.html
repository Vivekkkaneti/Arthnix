FAQs

Income tax calculator FAQs.

Answers to common questions about the calculator and its estimates.

What is the difference between the old and new tax regimes?

The new regime generally uses revised slab rates with fewer deductions. The old regime offers eligible deductions and exemptions but uses different slab rates. The result for an individual depends on income, age, deductions, exemptions and applicable tax rules.

Does this calculator include the standard deduction?

Yes. The standard deduction is entered separately and is deducted from the selected regime's income calculation. The default is ₹75,000. Confirm the amount permitted for your income type and applicable tax year.

Can I use this calculator if I have deductions under section 80C?

Yes. Enter eligible section 80C amounts and other deductions in their respective fields. The calculator applies the old-regime deductions only to the old-regime estimate. Eligibility and limits depend on the applicable law and your circumstances.

Why might my actual tax differ from the estimate?

Your actual liability may include special-rate income, capital gains, surcharge marginal relief, income adjustments, tax credits, TDS, advance tax, loss restrictions or other provisions not fully represented by this simplified calculator.

Does this tool file my income tax return?

No. This is an estimation tool only. It does not submit an income tax return, save your return with the government, or replace official filing software.

Is the calculator free?

Yes. The calculator is free to use and does not require an account. Your calculations run in your browser.
