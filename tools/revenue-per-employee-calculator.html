Frequently Asked Questions

A few things to know.

Understand the calculation, how to interpret the result, and what to consider when comparing businesses.

What is revenue per employee?
Revenue per employee is a business metric calculated by dividing total revenue for a reporting period by the number of employees used in that calculation. It describes the relationship between revenue and workforce size.
How do I calculate revenue per employee?
Divide total revenue by employee count. For example, if annual revenue is ₹50,00,000 and the average employee count is 25, revenue per employee is ₹2,00,000 for the year.
Should I use average or current employee count?
Average headcount over the reporting period can be useful when employee numbers change during the year. Using only the current headcount may produce a different result. Choose a method appropriate for your reporting purpose and apply it consistently when comparing periods.
Is revenue per employee the same as profit per employee?
No. Revenue is the income generated from business activities before subtracting expenses. Profit reflects revenue after applicable costs. A company can have high revenue per employee and still have low profit margins or operating losses.
What is a good revenue per employee?
There is no single target that applies to every business. Results depend on the industry, business model, pricing, workforce structure, outsourcing, capital intensity, and reporting method. Compare companies cautiously and consider other relevant financial measures.
Can I compare monthly and annual revenue per employee?
You can compare the metrics when each calculation uses revenue and employee count from matching periods. Seasonal businesses may show substantial monthly variation. An annual figure can provide a broader view, while shorter periods can help identify changes during the year.
Does this calculator store my business figures?
The calculation runs in your browser using the values you enter on this page. This calculator code does not send those values to a server or save them in browser storage. Normal website hosting and analytics services, if present elsewhere on the website, may have their own data practices.
