How Are Working Days Calculated?

Working days are the days in a selected date range that remain after excluding the weekend days and applicable holidays you specify. This calculator supports different weekend schedules and date-inclusion rules.

Working Days = Calendar Days − Non-working Days Non-working days include selected weekends and holidays that fall on otherwise working days.

For example, when Saturday and Sunday are weekends, a Monday-to-Friday range usually contains five working days, provided none of those dates is excluded as a holiday.

Example Calculation

Suppose you select Monday, 5 October 2026 through Friday, 9 October 2026, inclusive, with Saturday and Sunday set as weekends.

  • Calendar days: 5
  • Weekend days: 0
  • Applicable holidays: 0
  • Working days: 5

If you add a holiday on Wednesday, 7 October, the result becomes four working days. The calculator uses the dates you enter and does not assume a particular country's official holiday calendar.

Frequently Asked Questions

Working Days Calculator FAQs

Understand how date ranges, weekends, and holidays affect your result.

What is a working day?

A working day is a day treated as a regular working day under your selected schedule. This calculator defines working days as dates that are not excluded by your selected weekend settings or holiday list.

Are public holidays automatically excluded?

No. Public holidays vary by country, state, region, and employer. Enter the applicable dates in the holiday field to exclude them from the calculation.

Can I calculate working days for a notice period?

Yes. Enter the start and end dates and choose the date-inclusion rule that matches your intended calculation. Employment contracts and local rules may define notice periods differently, so verify the applicable terms.

Does the calculator count the start and end dates?

By default, both dates are included. You can choose to exclude the start date, the end date, or both dates using the date-range calculation selector.

Can I change the weekend days?

Yes. Select any combination of weekdays as non-working days. If your schedule has no weekend days, uncheck all seven options. The calculator will then count all non-holiday dates as working days.

Can I save the result?

Use Copy Results to copy a text summary, or select Print / Save PDF to print the calculation or save it as a PDF through your browser's print dialog.
