Understand Your Pay

What makes up variable pay?

Variable pay is compensation that can change based on individual, team, company, or sales performance. It is commonly offered in addition to fixed salary, but eligibility and payout conditions vary by employer and compensation plan.

Use this tool to estimate potential earnings. Always compare your calculation with the written terms of your actual offer or incentive plan.

Fixed pay

The fixed salary component for the selected period. This calculator does not independently determine CTC, take-home salary, or statutory deductions.

Target variable pay

The incentive amount associated with achieving a defined performance target. It may be stated as a fixed amount or a percentage of fixed pay.

Target achievement

Your measured performance expressed as a percentage of the assigned target. Employer plans may use different metrics or rules for determining achievement.

Payout multiplier

An adjustment applied to the achievement-based payout. A multiplier may vary with performance tiers or other conditions in an employer's compensation plan.

Commission and other incentives

Additional eligible earnings, such as sales commissions, spot bonuses, or other incentive payments. Their tax treatment and payment conditions depend on the applicable rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers.
Better planning.

Learn how the calculator treats performance bonuses, commissions, and estimated total compensation.

What is variable pay?
Variable pay is compensation that depends on factors such as individual performance, sales results, team targets, or company performance. Examples include performance bonuses, sales commissions, and incentive payments.
How is performance-based variable pay calculated?
This calculator multiplies target variable pay by target achievement and the payout multiplier. Both percentages are entered as percentage values and converted into multipliers for the calculation. This is a simplified model, not a universal employer payout formula.
Can I calculate variable pay as a percentage of salary?
Yes. Select "Percentage of fixed pay" and enter the target variable-pay percentage. For example, a target of 20% on fixed pay of ₹6,00,000 produces target variable pay of ₹1,20,000 for that same period.
Does the result represent take-home salary?
No. The result is estimated compensation before taxes and deductions, unless you have entered values that already account for them. It does not calculate income tax, provident fund, professional tax, or other payroll deductions.
Does the calculator account for bonus caps or eligibility rules?
No. The calculator does not automatically apply employer-specific thresholds, caps, eligibility requirements, clawbacks, or performance tiers. Review your compensation plan and adjust the inputs to reflect applicable rules where possible.
Can I use the calculator for monthly compensation?
Yes. Select Monthly and enter monthly fixed pay and monthly incentive targets. All amounts should use the same period. The calculator does not automatically convert monthly inputs into annual figures.
Are my salary details uploaded or saved?
This page performs its calculations in your browser and does not include a server submission or account-saving feature. Downloaded summaries are generated locally. Be mindful of sensitive information when saving or sharing your results.
Is the calculated incentive guaranteed?
No. This is an estimate based on the values you enter. Actual payments depend on your employer's written plan, measured performance, eligibility, approval, and applicable policies.
