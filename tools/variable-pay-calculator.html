Understand Your Pay

What makes up variable compensation?

Variable pay is compensation linked to performance, results, sales, or specific business outcomes. It is usually paid in addition to fixed salary and can vary by employer and incentive plan.

01

Performance bonus

A bonus linked to individual, team, or company performance. The amount may depend on ratings, target achievement, and payout rules.

02

Sales commission

Additional earnings linked to sales, revenue, collections, or closed deals. Some plans use a fixed percentage while others use tiers.

03

Target variable pay

The amount you may receive at 100% target achievement, subject to eligibility and the conditions in your employer's plan.

Common Questions

Variable pay calculator FAQs

Learn how to interpret the results and use them for compensation planning.

What is variable pay?

Variable pay is compensation that depends on performance, targets, sales, business results, or other conditions. Common examples include annual performance bonuses, sales commissions, incentives, and target-linked payouts. Unlike fixed pay, the amount may change between periods.

How do I calculate variable pay from target achievement?

Under a simple linear plan, divide actual achievement by the target and multiply the result by target variable pay. For example, if target variable pay is ₹1,00,000 and achievement is 110%, the uncapped payout is ₹1,10,000. Thresholds, multipliers, and caps may change the final amount.

What is the difference between fixed pay and variable pay?

Fixed pay is the salary component generally paid according to your employment terms. Variable pay depends on the conditions of an incentive or bonus plan. Your offer letter may define these components differently, so review the employer's compensation structure before comparing offers.

Is variable pay guaranteed?

Not necessarily. Some plans require a minimum performance level, active employment on a specified date, manager approval, or company-level results. Eligibility and payout conditions vary by employer. Check your offer letter and incentive policy to understand what is guaranteed and what is conditional.

Does this calculator calculate income tax automatically?

No. You can enter an estimated deduction manually, but the calculator does not determine your tax liability. Actual tax treatment depends on your income, tax regime, applicable law, payroll details, and other circumstances.

Can I use this calculator for monthly or quarterly incentives?

Yes. Choose the relevant compensation period and enter fixed pay and target variable pay for that same period. Ensure that your performance target, actual achievement, commissions, incentives, and deductions are also entered for a consistent period.

Is my compensation data uploaded anywhere?

The calculations on this page run in your browser. This page's calculator code does not submit your compensation inputs to a backend. Standard website hosting or analytics services, if configured elsewhere on the website, may have their own data practices.
