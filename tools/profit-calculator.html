Understand Your Results

Know the numbers behind your business.

Profit is more than the difference between the buying and selling price. Additional expenses also affect how much your business actually earns.

01

Total sales revenue

Revenue is the amount generated by selling your products before deducting the costs included in this calculator.

Formula: Selling price per unit × Quantity sold
02

Net profit or loss

Subtract the product costs and additional order expenses from sales revenue. A positive result indicates profit; a negative result indicates a loss.

Formula: Revenue − Product costs − Additional expenses
03

Profit margin

Profit margin shows the portion of revenue that remains as profit after deducting the costs entered here.

Formula: Net profit ÷ Revenue × 100
04

Markup percentage

Markup compares profit against total costs. It helps you understand how much you earn relative to what the order costs your business.

Formula: Net profit ÷ Total cost × 100
05

Break-even selling price

This is the average selling price per unit needed to recover the product costs and additional expenses entered for the order. It does not include costs you have omitted.

Formula: Total order cost ÷ Quantity sold
