Understand your numbers

How profit is calculated

Understanding the difference between profit, margin, and markup helps you price products more confidently.

01

Net profit or loss

Net profit is the amount left after subtracting your product costs and additional expenses from your total revenue. A negative result means your costs exceed your revenue.

Net profit = Revenue − Total costs
02

Profit margin vs. markup

Profit margin measures profit against revenue. Markup measures profit against total costs. They use different denominators, so their percentages can differ.

Margin = Profit ÷ Revenue × 100
Markup = Profit ÷ Total costs × 100
03

Break-even selling price

This is the average selling price per unit needed to cover all costs entered for the order. Selling above this price produces a profit, assuming the entered costs remain accurate.

Break-even price = Total costs ÷ Quantity
