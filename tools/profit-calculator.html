UNDERSTAND YOUR NUMBERS

Profit is more than the money left over.

Understand the numbers behind your sales so you can set prices, manage expenses, and make more informed business decisions.

01

Net profit or loss

Net profit is the money remaining after subtracting all included costs from your revenue. If your costs exceed your revenue, the calculator displays a loss.

Revenue − Total costs = Net profit

02

Profit margin vs. markup

Profit margin measures profit against revenue. Markup measures profit against total cost. Both are percentages, but they use different reference amounts.

Margin = Profit ÷ Revenue
Markup = Profit ÷ Total cost

03

Break-even selling price

Your break-even price is the average selling price per unit required to cover the product costs and additional expenses entered in the calculator.

Total costs ÷ Quantity = Break-even price
