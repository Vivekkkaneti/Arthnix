Frequently asked questions

Salary slip generator FAQs.

How is net salary calculated?

Net salary payable is calculated as gross earnings minus total deductions. Gross earnings are the sum of the earning fields, and total deductions are the sum of the deduction fields you enter.

Does this tool automatically calculate PF, ESI, or income tax?

No. These deductions depend on the employee's circumstances, applicable rules, and payroll policy. Enter the correct amounts supplied by your payroll team or calculated using the relevant official rules.

Can I download the salary slip as a PDF?

Yes. Select Print / PDF, then choose “Save as PDF” in your browser's print dialog. The print layout is designed to include the salary slip rather than the form.

Is this an official or verified salary slip?

No. This tool creates a formatted document using user-entered information. It does not verify employment, salary, or deductions, and it does not issue a company-certified payslip. Use it only when you are authorized to prepare the document.

Are my salary details uploaded or saved?

This page performs calculations in your browser and does not contain a server submission feature. Avoid using the tool on shared devices, and close the page when you finish. Browser extensions, device monitoring, or your browser environment may have their own data-handling behavior.
