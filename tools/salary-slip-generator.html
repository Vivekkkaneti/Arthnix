Common Questions

Salary slip generator
FAQs.

Learn how the generator works and what to verify before using a salary slip for official purposes.

Is this salary slip generator free?

Yes. You can enter salary details, view the generated salary slip, and print or save it as a PDF using your browser.

How is net salary calculated?

Net salary is calculated by adding the earnings you enter and subtracting the deductions you enter. The calculation is gross earnings minus total deductions. It does not automatically calculate tax or statutory payroll contributions.

Can I save the salary slip as a PDF?

Yes. Select Print / Save PDF and choose your browser's Save as PDF option in the print dialog. Check the preview and page settings before saving.

Does this tool calculate PF, TDS, or professional tax automatically?

No. You must enter the deduction amounts yourself. Actual deductions may depend on applicable laws, salary structure, employee eligibility, employer policy, and other circumstances.

Is the generated salary slip an official payslip?

The tool creates a formatted document from the details you provide. It does not verify employment, authenticate payroll records, or certify the document. The employer or authorized payroll team should verify and issue official salary slips.

Are my salary details uploaded?

The form calculations and preview run in your browser. This page does not contain code that uploads the entered salary details to a server. As with any website, external services used by the browser, such as font loading, have their own privacy practices.
