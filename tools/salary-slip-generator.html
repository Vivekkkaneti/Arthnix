Understand the numbers

How is net salary calculated?

The generator uses the earnings and deductions you enter to calculate the amount payable for the selected salary period.

+

Total earnings

Basic salary + house rent allowance + special allowance + other earnings.

−

Total deductions

Provident fund + professional tax + income tax / TDS + other deductions.

=

Net salary payable

Total earnings − total deductions. The result is the estimated take-home amount based on the figures entered.

Payroll accuracy: Tax, provident fund, professional tax, leave adjustments, and other deductions depend on applicable rules and the employee's circumstances. Enter the actual values supplied by your payroll team rather than relying on example amounts.
