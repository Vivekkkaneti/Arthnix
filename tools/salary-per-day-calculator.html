FAQs

Salary per day calculator FAQs.

Common questions about calculating daily and hourly salary.

How do I calculate salary per day from monthly salary?

Divide your monthly salary by the number of days used for the calculation. For example, ₹30,000 divided by 26 equals approximately ₹1,153.85 per day. Dividing by 30 gives ₹1,000 per day.

Why does the 26-day method give a higher daily salary?

The same monthly salary is divided by fewer days, so the calculated daily amount is higher. The 26-day method is a common estimate, but the correct divisor for a payroll or legal calculation depends on the applicable rules and employment terms.

How is hourly salary calculated?

This calculator divides your estimated daily salary by the number of working hours you enter. If your daily salary is ₹1,200 and you work 8 hours, the estimated hourly equivalent is ₹150.

Is daily salary the same as take-home pay per day?

Not necessarily. If you enter gross salary, the result is based on gross salary. If you enter your take-home salary, the result represents a take-home equivalent. The calculator does not apply tax, provident fund, insurance, or other payroll deductions.

Can I calculate salary using a custom number of days?

Yes. Enter a number from 1 to 31 in the custom working days field. This value overrides the selected 26-day or 30-day method for the daily and hourly calculations.

Does the calculator account for holidays and paid leave?

No. It provides a salary equivalent based on the values you enter. It does not adjust for public holidays, paid leave, unpaid leave, overtime, or employer-specific payroll policies.

Is this salary calculator free?

Yes. You can use the ARTHNIX salary per day calculator without creating an account. The calculations run in your browser.
