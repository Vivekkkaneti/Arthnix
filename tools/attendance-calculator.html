Attendance percentage formula

The basic formula is:

Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes Held) × 100

For example, if you attend 72 out of 90 classes, your attendance is 80%. Institutions may apply their own rules for medical leave, practicals, labs, condonation, or minimum attendance requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Attendance calculator FAQs

Understand the calculations and use the results to plan your attendance.

How do I calculate my attendance percentage?

Divide the number of classes attended by the total number of classes held, then multiply by 100. For example, 45 attended classes out of 60 gives 75% attendance.

How many classes must I attend to reach 75%?

The answer depends on your current attended classes and total classes held. This calculator estimates how many consecutive classes you must attend to reach your selected target without missing any in between.

How many classes can I miss and still maintain 75% attendance?

It depends on your current attendance and the number of future classes. The calculator estimates the maximum future absences you can have while remaining at or above your target, assuming the future class count you enter is accurate.

Can I use this for school, college, or coaching classes?

Yes. You can use the calculator for school, college, university, coaching classes, training sessions, or other programs where attendance is measured as sessions attended divided by sessions held.

Does the monthly tracker update the main calculator?

No. The monthly tracker is separate from the main calculator. It summarizes dates you mark as Present or Absent for the selected month. The main calculator uses the numbers entered in its own fields.

Does this tool save my attendance records?

The tool runs in your browser and does not require an account. Tracker entries are not uploaded to a cloud account and are not guaranteed to persist if you reload or close the page.

Is 75% attendance compulsory everywhere?

No universal rule applies to every institution and course. Requirements can vary by institution, university, program, and applicable regulations. Confirm the required percentage with your institution.
