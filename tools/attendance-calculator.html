FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

Helpful details about using the employee attendance calculator.

How is employee attendance percentage calculated?

Divide the employee's present days by the total scheduled working days for the period, then multiply by 100. For example, 20 present days out of 22 scheduled working days equals 90.91%.

Should weekly offs and public holidays be included?

Enter scheduled working days after applying the company's calendar and applicable holidays. Do not count non-working days as absences unless your employer's policy specifically requires it.

Are approved leave days counted as present?

This calculator reports leave separately and does not automatically add leave days to present days. Whether approved leave qualifies toward attendance requirements depends on your organization's policy.

Does the calculator determine salary or payroll deductions?

No. It calculates attendance figures only. Salary deductions, paid leave, loss of pay, overtime, and payable days can depend on employment terms and applicable rules. Use the relevant payroll policy before making payroll decisions.

What does “unaccounted scheduled days” mean?

It is the number of scheduled working days remaining after subtracting present days, absent days, leave days, and separately entered credited holidays or offs. If the figure is negative, the entered categories exceed scheduled working days and should be reviewed.

Can I save or share the attendance summary?

Use Copy Summary to copy the calculated figures, or select Print / Save PDF to open your browser's print dialog. You can choose a printer or a PDF destination if your device supports it.
