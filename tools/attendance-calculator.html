How Is Attendance Calculated?

Understand your attendance percentage and make a plan to meet your institution's attendance requirement.

01

Enter your class totals

Add the total number of classes conducted and the number of classes you attended. Your missed classes are calculated automatically.

02

Set your attendance target

Choose the required percentage, such as 75% or 80%. The calculator estimates how many consecutive future classes you must attend to reach that target.

03

Plan your upcoming classes

Enter the number of future classes you expect. See your projected attendance if you attend all of them, and use the monthly tracker to record attendance.

Attendance Percentage Formula (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes Held) × 100
