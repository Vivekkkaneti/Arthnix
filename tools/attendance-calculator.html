Calculation method

Understand the formula.

The calculator uses your attendance entries to estimate attendance percentage and payable days.

Attendance percentage

Full present days count as one attendance day, while half-days count as 0.5. The result is divided by the selected attendance denominator and multiplied by 100.

Attendance-equivalent days = Present days + (Half-days × 0.5)
Attendance % = Attendance-equivalent days ÷ Attendance denominator × 100

Estimated payable days

This calculator estimates payable days as attendance-equivalent days plus paid leave. Your employer may use a different payroll formula, so treat this figure as an estimate.

Payable days = Attendance-equivalent days + Paid leave days
Unpaid leave is not added to payable days.
