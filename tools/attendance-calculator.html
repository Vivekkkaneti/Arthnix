Calculation Method

How attendance is calculated

Attendance rate uses attendance-equivalent days divided by the selected attendance denominator. Half-days count as half of a full attendance day.

Attendance-equivalent days = Full present days + (Half-days × 0.5)
Attendance rate = Attendance-equivalent days ÷ Attendance denominator × 100
Estimated payable days = Full present days + (Half-days × 0.5) + Paid leave days

Attendance denominator: scheduled working days minus paid leave when paid leave is excluded from the rate; otherwise, scheduled working days. A zero denominator produces a 0% display with an explanatory note. This is a general calculator, not a statement of any specific company's payroll or legal policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Employee attendance, explained.

Answers to common questions about attendance percentages, half-days, leave, and payable-day estimates.

How do I calculate an employee's attendance percentage?

Divide attendance-equivalent days by the applicable attendance denominator and multiply by 100. Each full present day counts as one day and each half-day counts as 0.5 day. The paid-leave setting controls whether paid leave is removed from the denominator. Follow your organization's official attendance policy.

Do half-days count toward attendance?

Yes. This calculator treats one half-day as 0.5 attendance day. For example, 18 full days and two half-days equal 19 attendance-equivalent days. Your employer may use different rules for shifts, late arrivals, or partial days.

What is the difference between paid and unpaid leave?

Paid leave is leave recorded as paid under the relevant company policy. Unpaid leave is recorded as leave without pay. This calculator reports the categories separately and includes paid leave in its estimated payable-day figure. Actual payroll treatment must be confirmed with your employer.

How are payable days calculated?

The estimate is full present days plus half-day equivalents plus paid leave days. It does not automatically account for salary components, statutory rules, overtime, shift allowances, deductions, or company-specific payroll adjustments.

Does this calculator automatically detect weekends and holidays?

No. Enter the scheduled working-day total from your official calendar. Weekend patterns, regional public holidays, company holidays, and shift schedules vary, so the tool does not assume a universal work calendar.

Can I use the attendance summary for payroll?

You can use it as a preliminary reference, but it should not replace your official attendance register or payroll system. Verify the input figures, leave approvals, shift rules, rounding rules, and applicable company policies before processing employee pay.

Can I save or share the result?

Use Copy Summary to copy a text summary, or Print / Save PDF to open your browser's print dialog. The availability of PDF saving depends on your browser and device.
