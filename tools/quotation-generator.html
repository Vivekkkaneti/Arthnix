Common Questions

Quotation Generator FAQs

Understand what this tool can do before preparing your quotation.

Is the ARTHNIX Quotation Generator free?

Yes. You can enter your details, add line items, preview the quotation and use your browser's print function to save it as a PDF.

Can I add GST to my quotation?

Yes. You can select a GST rate for each item and choose intra-state, inter-state or no-GST treatment. Verify the correct tax treatment and rates for your particular transaction before sharing the document.

Can I include my company logo and signature?

Yes. You can upload a logo and an optional signature image. The images are used locally to customise the quotation preview.

Is a quotation the same as a tax invoice?

No. A quotation generally communicates proposed prices and terms before an order is confirmed. It is not a substitute for a tax invoice or other document required by applicable law.

Are my quotation details uploaded to ARTHNIX?

This page generates the quotation in your browser and does not send its form details to an ARTHNIX backend. Your browser may still retain information according to your browser settings, and any external service you use separately is governed by its own policies.
