Frequently asked questions

Learn how to use the quotation generator.

Is this quotation generator free?

Yes. You can create, customize, preview and print quotations using this tool without paying for the generator.

Can I add my business logo?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG or WebP logo from your device. The logo appears in the quotation preview and printed document.

Can I change the quotation's colour?

Yes. Use the accent colour picker in the quotation information section. The heading, item table and total section will use your selected colour.

How do I download my quotation as a PDF?

Select Print / Save PDF. In the browser print dialog, choose Save as PDF as the destination, then save the file.

Are GST and discounts calculated automatically?

Yes. Choose a tax mode and GST rate, then select a percentage or fixed discount if needed. The totals update automatically. Verify the final figures and tax treatment before sending a quotation to your customer.

Is my quotation saved automatically?

This tool creates the quotation in your current browser session. Print or save a PDF to keep a copy. Avoid refreshing the page before saving work you need.
