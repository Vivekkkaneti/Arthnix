What does this calculator include?

The calculator supports several common incentive structures. You can adjust the rates and thresholds to reflect your own company policy.

  • Percentage-based sales commission.
  • Target achievement and threshold-based bonuses.
  • Fixed performance bonuses.
  • Progressive commission across three sales tiers.
  • Additional earnings and deductions.

Understand your estimate

The result is based entirely on the figures and rules entered. It does not establish your legal entitlement to an incentive or determine the terms of an employment agreement.

Incentive policies may use eligible revenue, collected payments, profit, returns, cancellations, quotas, or other conditions. Confirm the applicable rules with your employer before using this estimate for payroll.

Frequently Asked Questions

Incentive calculation,
explained.

Understand how the calculator handles common incentive structures.

How is a sales commission calculated?

For percentage-based commission, the calculator multiplies eligible sales by the entered commission rate. For example, ₹1,00,000 in eligible sales at 5% produces a base commission of ₹5,000, provided the configured minimum achievement threshold is met.

How does the target achievement bonus work?

The calculator divides eligible sales by the sales target to determine achievement. If achievement meets or exceeds the threshold you enter, the configured target bonus is included. If the target is zero, the achievement percentage is shown as unavailable rather than dividing by zero.

What is a tiered sales commission?

A progressive tiered commission applies different rates to different portions of eligible sales. For example, the first rate applies to sales within Tier 1, the second rate applies to sales above the first limit up to the second limit, and the third rate applies to sales above the second limit.

Can I calculate incentives in Indian rupees?

Yes. All monetary amounts are displayed in Indian Rupees (₹) using Indian number formatting. Enter all monetary values in the same currency for a meaningful result.

Can I print or save the incentive calculation?

Yes. Choose Print Summary to open your browser's print dialogue. From there, select a printer or choose Save as PDF if your browser and device provide that option. Export CSV creates a spreadsheet-compatible summary of the calculation.

Does the calculator automatically calculate tax?

No. It does not determine income tax, payroll withholding, statutory contributions, or other mandatory deductions. Enter only deductions established by your applicable payroll rules and verify them with your payroll team.

Are my employee details uploaded anywhere?

The calculator performs its calculations locally in your browser and does not submit your entered employee or sales details to ARTHNIX. Avoid entering sensitive personal or confidential business information on devices you do not control.
