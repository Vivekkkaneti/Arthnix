Frequently asked questions

Incentive calculator FAQs.

Understand the methods used by this calculator before relying on the estimate.

What is an employee incentive?

An employee incentive is additional compensation linked to a performance goal, sales target, productivity measure, or other criteria established by an employer. It may be paid in addition to fixed salary, subject to company policy.

How is a sales commission calculated?

For the commission method in this tool, the estimate is actual sales multiplied by the commission rate. For example, ₹2,00,000 in eligible sales at 5% produces a ₹10,000 estimated commission. Your employer may use eligible sales, collected revenue, slabs, caps, or other rules instead.

How does target achievement work?

Target achievement is calculated as actual sales divided by the sales target, multiplied by 100. A zero target is treated as zero achievement. In the fixed target-based method, the target incentive is awarded only when your achievement meets the minimum percentage you entered.

Can I calculate an incentive based on profit?

Yes. Select the profit commission method, enter actual profit, and provide the applicable commission rate. This tool does not verify which costs or adjustments your company includes when calculating eligible profit.

Does the calculator determine tax or statutory deductions?

No. You can enter an optional estimated deduction percentage applied to the incentive and any other deductions you want to account for. This is not a tax calculator and does not determine your actual income tax, TDS, PF, ESI, or other statutory liability.

Can I save the result as a PDF?

Yes. Select “Print / Save PDF” and choose “Save as PDF” in your browser's print dialog. The printed statement is an estimate based on the information entered, not an employer-verified compensation statement.
