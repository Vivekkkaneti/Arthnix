Calculation Method

Clear inputs.
Transparent estimates.

The calculator uses the rates and rules you provide. It does not assume that every organization calculates incentives in the same way.

Important: This calculator provides an estimate only. Check your employment agreement, company incentive policy, eligible revenue rules, refund adjustments, and approval requirements before finalizing a payout. Tax treatment may vary depending on your circumstances.

Target achievement

Calculated as actual sales divided by the sales target, multiplied by 100. If the target is zero, the calculator displays achievement as not applicable.

Sales commission

The commission rate is applied either to all actual sales or only to sales above the target, depending on the selected commission basis. Commission is applied only when the achievement threshold is met.

Gross and net incentive

Gross incentive is the sales commission plus the fixed and additional bonuses. The estimated net incentive is the gross incentive minus entered adjustments or deductions, with a minimum displayed payout of zero.

Company-specific incentive policies

Some plans use tiered rates, product-specific commissions, collection targets, caps, returns, or manager approvals. Enter figures according to your plan; this tool does not automatically determine those rules.

Your data and privacy

Calculations are performed in your browser. This page does not send the details you enter to ARTHNIX through the calculator.
