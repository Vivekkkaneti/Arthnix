01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ARTHNIX ("ARTHNIX," "we," "our," or "us") may collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website or voluntarily contact us.

By using the website or choosing to share information with us, you acknowledge this policy. Where applicable law requires consent or another legal basis for a particular activity, we will rely on the appropriate basis rather than treating website use alone as consent.

This policy may need to be supplemented when additional products, account features, integrations, or services become available.

02 Information You Provide

We may receive information that you voluntarily choose to provide when communicating with us or enquiring about ARTHNIX.

This may include:

  • Contact information: such as your name and email address.
  • Business information: such as your company or business name, if you choose to provide it.
  • Enquiry details: such as your subject, message, selected enquiry type, or information about a product or plan you are interested in.
  • Other information: any additional details you choose to include in a message.

Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, confidential business records, or other sensitive information unless a specific service expressly requires it and appropriate safeguards have been established.

Contact form: If you use a contact form that opens a prefilled email in your email application, the information is not automatically delivered to us merely because you submit the form. You must send the email from your email application for us to receive it. Your email provider may process the message according to its own policies.

03 How Information Is Used

Information you choose to provide may be used for reasonable purposes connected with your interaction with ARTHNIX, including:

  • Responding to your questions and enquiries.
  • Communicating with you about products, services, demonstrations, or requests you initiate.
  • Understanding feedback and improving the website and planned products.
  • Identifying and addressing technical issues, misuse, or security concerns.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations and protecting legitimate rights.

We do not intend to use information for purposes unrelated to the context in which it was provided without an appropriate basis or notice where required by law.

04 Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information as part of the website's stated purpose. We may disclose information in limited circumstances where necessary and lawful, such as:

  • Service providers: providers that support website hosting, communications, security, or other services, if and when we use them to process relevant information.
  • Legal requirements: where disclosure is required by law, regulation, court order, or a valid request from an authorised public authority.
  • Protection of rights: where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, address security issues, or protect users, our services, or other people.
  • Business changes: in connection with a proposed or completed restructuring, acquisition, or transfer, subject to applicable legal requirements.

If a future ARTHNIX feature requires sharing information with an integration, AI provider, or another service, the relevant details should be explained before or when that feature is used, as required by law.

05 Data Retention

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining appropriate records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information, the reason it was collected, and applicable legal requirements.

We cannot promise a specific deletion timeline for information held by independent email providers or other third parties. Their retention practices are governed by their own policies and systems.

06 Security

We recognise the importance of protecting information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. Appropriate safeguards should be implemented based on the nature of the information and the services being operated.

No website, email system, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please use care when deciding what information to send online.

As ARTHNIX develops additional features, its security practices and safeguards should be reviewed and updated to reflect the actual systems in use.
