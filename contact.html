Quick answers

Before you reach out

Here are a few helpful details about ARTHNIX and our business tools.

What is ARTHNIX?

ARTHNIX is building accessible business tools and intelligent systems designed to simplify everyday business workflows. Explore the available tools through our website.

How do I report a problem with a tool?

Choose “Technical support” in the enquiry form. Include the tool name, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Avoid sharing sensitive information.

Can my business discuss a partnership?

Yes. Select “Partnership” or “Business enquiry” and describe your proposed collaboration, integration, or business requirement.

How soon will I receive a response?

Response times may vary depending on the type and volume of enquiries. We do not promise a specific response time.

Can I suggest a new feature?

Absolutely. Select “Feedback or suggestion” and explain the problem you are trying to solve or the improvement you would like to see.

Is submitting this form a support guarantee?

No. Submitting an enquiry does not create a service agreement or guarantee a particular outcome. Any commitments will be communicated separately where applicable.
