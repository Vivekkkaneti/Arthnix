A few quick answers

Before you reach out.

A few things that may help you prepare your enquiry.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Briefly explain who you are, what you need, the outcome you're looking for, and any relevant deadlines or requirements. Avoid including confidential or sensitive information.

What is included in the Free plan?

The proposed Free plan is ₹0 forever and is intended as a starting point for individuals and small businesses. It is planned to include one business workspace, basic GST calculations, limited monthly invoice creation, basic business tools, reports, and one user per workspace. Features and usage limits must be confirmed before launch.

What is the proposed price of Pro?

The proposed Pro plan is ₹299 per month. It is intended to offer everything in Free, plus higher invoice limits, expanded saved-invoice capacity, advanced reports, additional payroll features, higher AI assistant usage, and additional export options. Billing is not yet available, and features and limits require confirmation.

What is the proposed price of Business?

The proposed Business plan is ₹799 per month. It is intended for teams managing multiple workflows or workspaces, with expanded storage, higher usage allowances, team-oriented tools, and additional team members. Pricing, team limits, and features must be confirmed before launch.

Can I enquire about a plan before subscribing?

Yes. Choose the relevant plan in the contact form and explain your questions or requirements. The prices and features are currently proposed. Paid subscriptions and billing are not yet available.

Can I contact ARTHNIX about a partnership?

Yes. Select “Business Partnership” in the enquiry type field and describe the proposed collaboration, the organisations involved, and what you'd like to explore.

Has my message been delivered when I submit this form?

This form opens a prepared email in the visitor's email application. The visitor must click Send in that application to deliver the enquiry. It does not send emails automatically or store submissions on a server.
